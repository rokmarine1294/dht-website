Our foundation

Built for asymmetric
returns.

Two Korean Marines. One shared conviction: that the principles which win on the battlefield — clarity, discipline, and structured execution — can be applied systematically to the markets.

01

Wait for Edge

The market is classified first — trending, ranging, or low volatility — using ADX and ATR. Then the EMA 50/200 trend filter must align. Only then does the entry trigger fire. Three layers must agree before a single trade is placed.

02

Let Winners Run

Stop loss is set at 1.5× ATR. At 1R profit the stop moves to breakeven — the trade can no longer lose money. Target is 2R. If the trend reverses or 10 candles pass without progress, the bot exits. Losses are capped. Winners run.

03

Control the Drawdown

A 20% drawdown requires a 25% gain just to break even. Every trade has an ATR-based hard stop. Once in profit the risk drops to zero. The system is designed to stay in the game long enough for the big moves to compound.

The system

Professional-grade
modular architecture.

Not a single script. Six specialised engines working in concert — the same approach used by quantitative trading firms.

🧠
Market Regime Intelligence

Uses ADX and ATR to classify the market as trending, ranging, or low volatility — then switches entry strategy accordingly. Breakout entries in trends, mean reversion in ranges, expansion entries in quiet markets. One bot, three modes.

ADX · ATR · Adaptive
🐋
EMA Trend Filter

Every entry must align with the long-term trend. EMA 50 above EMA 200 — long bias only. EMA 50 below EMA 200 — short bias only. Even a valid signal is rejected if it fights the structure. Trend-following at the core.

EMA 50 · EMA 200
🛡
Structured Exit System

Stop loss at 1.5× ATR from entry. At 1R profit the stop moves to breakeven — no further loss is possible. Target is 2R. If the EMA trend reverses or 10 candles pass without progress, the bot exits immediately. Clean, rules-based, no discretion.

Breakeven · 2R target · Time exit
⚡
ATR Trailing Stop

Once profit exceeds 1.5R the stop trails dynamically using ATR — locking in gains as the move extends. If the EMA trend structure reverses at any point, the position closes immediately. No second-guessing the signal.

Trails at 1.5R
📊
Dynamic Position Sizing

Position size is calculated from the ATR-based stop distance — risk is fixed per trade regardless of market conditions. The system scales with your account, not with emotion. Consistent exposure, consistent discipline.

ATR-based sizing

Common questions

Everything you
need to know.

Is my money safe? +

Your funds never leave your exchange account. The bot only requires read + trade API permissions — it physically cannot withdraw or transfer your money. You remain in full custody of your capital at all times. We recommend starting with an amount you are comfortable testing with.

What exchanges does it support? +

Currently fully supported on Bitget USDT-M Futures. Binance and OKX compatibility is in active development. We recommend Bitget for beta testers as it has been the most thoroughly tested environment.

How much capital do I need? +

The bot is designed to work with accounts from $100 USD upwards. Our beta testing has been conducted on a ~$200 AUD account. We recommend starting small to understand how the system behaves before scaling up. More capital does not mean better results — position sizing is dynamic.

How do I set it up? +

Setup takes under 30 minutes. You create a Bitget account, generate a read + trade API key (no withdrawal permissions needed), configure the bot with your credentials, and run it with a single command. A full step-by-step guide is provided to all beta testers and subscribers.

Does it trade 24/7 automatically? +

Yes. Once running the bot operates continuously, analysing markets, detecting regime shifts, and executing trades without any manual input. You receive an automated performance report via Telegram every 6 hours so you always know what's happening.

What is market regime detection? +

Most bots run one strategy regardless of market conditions — which means they perform well in trending markets but bleed in ranging or volatile ones. Our regime detection engine identifies the current market state and automatically switches strategy to match. This is one of the core advantages of the Horizon Bot.

Can I lose money? +

Yes. Cryptocurrency trading carries significant risk and losses are possible. The bot includes a risk engine with dynamic position sizing and exposure limits, but no system eliminates risk entirely. Never trade with capital you cannot afford to lose. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

How do I join the beta? +

Join our Discord community and follow the instructions in the #beta-testing-instruction channel. You will need to complete an NDA and agreement form. Spots are limited — we keep the beta cohort small to ensure we can provide proper support to every tester.

What does 1R and 2R mean? +

R stands for Risk — the amount you risk on a single trade. If you risk $10 on a trade, 1R = $10 profit, 2R = $20 profit, 4R = $40 profit. The bot uses this to manage exits systematically: at 1R it moves the stop to breakeven (no more loss possible), at 2R and 4R it takes partial profits, and holds the rest as a runner to maximise the move.

What is the time-based exit? +

If a trade is open for 10 candles without reaching target, the bot exits regardless of profit or loss. A stalled trade ties up capital and signals the thesis was wrong. The bot cuts it, frees the capital, and waits for the next clean setup. Opportunity cost is a real cost.
